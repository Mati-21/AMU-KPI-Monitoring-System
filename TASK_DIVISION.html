Module 05 • Backend/modules/05-contract-cascading
🎯 Contracts & KPI Cascading
Carries KPIs down the hierarchy. Each office has one contract per year with the office above, which sets its official baseline, target and weight. Offices record how they split responsibility, and differences are flagged, not corrected. Approved values change only through a change request, which adds a new version, so history stays traceable.
