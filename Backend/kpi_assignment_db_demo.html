💡 Key Takeaway: What happens in DB during Dispatch?

When a leader dispatches a KPI, the system writes a new record into the UserKPIs table. It links the UserId (recipient child user) and CreatedBy (dispatching parent user), storing the assigned baseline, target, and cycle year. The report table then reads from UserKPIs to list all assigned KPIs even before any quarterly results are logged into UserKPIResults!
